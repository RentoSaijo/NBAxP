    "Expected Points (xP) estimates the value of every shot by predicting how likely it is to be made given its context (e.g., distance, shot type, and situation) and then converting that probability into expected points (2× for twos, 3× for threes). By focusing on the quality of chances rather than whether shots happened to go in, xP helps show which teams consistently create high-value opportunities and where on the floor they do it. Check out our predictions for the 2025-26 season by selecting 2 teams to compare from the dropdown menu and hovering over the region you’re interested in! If you’re curious as to how exactly these values are calculated, check out the linked README upon clicking the GitHub logo!"
